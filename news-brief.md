# News brief — Tuesday 24 November 2026

The six stories pictured in `entry.png`, each verified against reporting from 24 November 2026.

1. **Huawei convicted in the US.** A federal jury in Brooklyn found Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts, including bank fraud and conspiracy to steal trade secrets, and acquitted it of racketeering conspiracy.
   Source: Reuters, "China's Huawei convicted in US in sprawling criminal trial over Iran business", 24 November 2026.

2. **Tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz.** Iran's IRGC Navy said four oil tankers exploded and caught fire after striking naval mines in and east of the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday; the claims were not independently verified.
   Source: ANI, "Four oil tankers explode, catch fire after hitting naval mines in Hormuz: Iran's Navy", 24 November 2026.

3. **Houthi drone hits Riyadh's tallest tower.** Yemen's Houthi fighters struck the Public Investment Fund Tower in Riyadh for the second time in less than a week, wounding at least 11 people, Saudi civil defence said.
   Source: Reuters, "Houthis hit Riyadh's tallest tower, 11 wounded, Saudi Civil defense says", 24 November 2026.

4. **Ukraine confirms strikes on Russian ships at sea.** President Zelenskyy confirmed on Tuesday that Ukraine was behind drone strikes on Russian ships: the cargo ship Sparta II, hit in the Gulf of Finland on Saturday, and the corvette Merkury, sunk with the cargo vessel MV Angara hit off Benghazi, Libya, on Sunday.
   Source: Reuters, "Ukraine insists attack on Russian ships in Libya was self-defence", 24 November 2026; Bloomberg via EuropeSays, "Zelenskyy Confirms Ukraine Hit Russian Ship in Gulf of Finland", 24 November 2026.

5. **Hamas security chief killed in Gaza.** An Israeli airstrike killed Naeem Abu Nahel, director of Hamas' internal security apparatus in Gaza, in western Gaza City on Tuesday, Hamas sources said.
   Source: Reuters, "Israeli airstrike kills senior Hamas security official in Gaza, Hamas sources say", 24 November 2026.

6. **Turkish military helicopter crashes.** Five Turkish soldiers were killed when an AS-532 helicopter of the Turkish Air Force crashed after taking off from Eskisehir for a training flight on Tuesday, the Defence Ministry said.
   Source: Reuters, "Five Turkish soldiers killed in helicopter crash, ministry says", 24 November 2026.
